# How to succeed in Incubyte: my TL;DR version

Thrive for excellence and always try to learn new things. Now, this is only possible if you have an amazing environment. So the goal is not just to improve ourselves but also to help others and take help when needed.

Use the **Radical Candor framework**: give feedback while being empathetic. This not only prevents hurting others' feelings but also helps us focus on what is more important: **“the problem.”** Use non-confrontational language.

Use the **“observation - impact - recommendations - discussion”** model.

**Observe** what went wrong. **State** its impact. Give **recommendations** or propose solutions, and finally **discuss** those solutions or ask for improvement.

## Community Engagement

The only way to create a good community is to have positive engagement.

Be active in meetings, keep the camera on, not just hear but listen to things, try to understand the problem, and propose how you can help or give feedback.

Engagement is not only limited to meetings, but learning as well. Join meetups, hackathons, learn new things, and then share them.

Learning something new with a community that’s also interested in learning that particular thing makes the learning **“interesting.”** That interest/engagement helps us maintain **“consistency”** (the only way to excel at anything) and also motivates us to learn more.

## Client Engagement

Understand their needs, their business, and the problems they face.

Understand how you can help them and remove those problems for them.

Be proactive in providing solutions and communication. These show that we care about them.

Caring builds trust. Trust makes us more reliable than others.

## Development

Be proactive here as well. Be observant of the current issues, even if they are minor. Just keep a note of them and the problems that they may cause in the future instead of waiting for the problem to happen and then reacting.
